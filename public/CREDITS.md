### Detail-study placeholders

The 40 files below are **not** independent Pexels photographs. Each was generated locally from the matching primary photograph by darkening and cropping it, then overlaying an explicit label reading “Back view / Photography to follow” (dresses) or “Suit pieces / Piece photography to follow” (suits). They exist so every card has a real, stable image path and an honest second state while verified same-garment photography is sourced.

- `assets/images/dresses/*-back.jpg` (20 files)
- `assets/images/suits/*-pieces.jpg` (20 files)

Replace a placeholder with a genuine matching photograph at the same path, then set `secondaryReady: true` on that product in `js/collections.js`. The visible labels switch from “View detail” to “View back” or “View pieces” automatically.
